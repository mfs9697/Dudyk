# Novelty and overlap positioning for IJF submission

This note is for the authors and the submission cover letter. It is not intended to be included in the manuscript verbatim.

## Present manuscript

**Problem:** branching initiation from the corner of a prescribed stationary V-shaped interfacial shear crack along a kinked interface.

**Outer field:** the corner singular field of the stationary V-shaped shear-crack configuration.

**Local model:** a small tensile process zone along a material-sector bisector.

**Main analytical content:** Mellin formulation, Wiener–Hopf solution, regularity condition, equilibrium zone length/opening/work, zone-related energy parameter, admissibility branches, residual singularity, and competing local fracture mechanisms.

## Relation to Kaminsky–Dudyk–Chornoivan, International Journal of Fracture 250:22 (2026)

That paper studies the initial kinking of a conventional interfacial crack and specifically the influence of T-stress on the process-zone solution near the crack tip.

The present paper differs in the base defect and singular field:
- the defect is a **V-shaped interfacial shear crack with contacting faces**;
- the branching site is its **corner**, not the tip of a conventional straight interfacial crack;
- the outer asymptotics are governed by the V-crack corner eigenfield;
- the physical-admissibility problem couples compressive crack-face contact with a tensile field in the material containing the zone;
- branch-tip interfacial propagation is treated as a competing local mechanism.

This is the most important distinction to explain to the IJF editor.

## Relation to Nazarenko–Kipnis, International Applied Mechanics 58:497–509

Nazarenko and Kipnis derive the analytical stationary V-shaped shear-crack field, determine the Mode-II intensity at the crack-branch tips, and study limiting equilibrium/interfacial propagation.

The present paper uses the stationary V-crack field as an **outer asymptotic input** and develops a different local problem:
- a tensile process zone at the corner;
- equilibrium process-zone characteristics;
- branching-initiation criteria from the corner.

Thus, the two works address different local fracture mechanisms of the same crack geometry.

## Relation to Kaminsky–Dudyk, Reports of the NAS of Ukraine (2026)

That work discusses crack-initiation criteria for an interfacial crack in a quasi-brittle material.

The present manuscript specializes and extends the criterion discussion to the V-shaped corner configuration and derives the corresponding analytical process-zone family. The energy quantity used here is explicitly interpreted as a **zone-related energy parameter** along that family, rather than as an independently established global contour integral.

## One-sentence novelty statement

> The paper provides a closed analytical process-zone model for branching initiation from the corner of a stationary V-shaped interfacial shear crack, linking the V-crack corner eigenfield to physically admissible process-zone states and to competing crack-branch-tip propagation.
