# Novelty positioning for editorial screening

Manuscript: **Analytical Model for Branching Initiation from the Corner of a V-Shaped Interfacial Shear Crack**

## Core contribution

The paper solves a local branching-initiation problem at the **corner of a prescribed stationary V-shaped interfacial shear crack**. The key modelling step is to use the singular field of the stationary contacting V-shaped crack as the outer asymptotics for a small tensile process zone placed along the bisector of one material sector.

The resulting problem is not a re-analysis of conventional interface-crack-tip kinking and not a repeat of the elastic limiting-equilibrium problem for the V-shaped shear crack.

## Relation to the 2026 IJF paper by Kaminsky, Dudyk, and Chornoivan

**Earlier problem**
- conventional interfacial crack;
- process zone associated with kinking from a crack tip;
- particular emphasis on the contribution of T-stress;
- direction and parameters of the crack-tip process zone.

**Present problem**
- stationary V-shaped interfacial shear crack with two contacting branches;
- process zone originates at the **corner point**, not at a conventional crack tip;
- the outer field is a corner eigenfield with an exponent controlled by geometry and elastic contrast;
- physical admissibility couples compressive contact on the V-shaped crack with tensile normal loading in the prospective branching material;
- derives equilibrium zone length, opening, work of separation, and a zone-related energy parameter;
- distinguishes corner branching from propagation at the crack-branch tips as competing mechanisms.

The overlap is methodological (process-zone modelling and Wiener–Hopf analysis), not duplication of the boundary-value problem or fracture mechanism.

## Relation to the 2022 Nazarenko–Kipnis papers

**Earlier problem**
- elastic field generated by interface shear cracks near the angular point;
- stress singularity of the stationary cracked configuration;
- Mode-II branch-tip stress intensity factor;
- limiting equilibrium and stability of interfacial crack propagation.

**Present problem**
- takes the stationary cracked elastic field as a known outer field;
- introduces a **new tensile process zone at the corner**;
- determines which material can host a physically admissible zone;
- obtains the equilibrium family of zone states;
- formulates branching initiation through critical opening or a zone-related energy parameter;
- examines the competition between corner branching and branch-tip interfacial propagation.

Thus the Nazarenko–Kipnis solution is an input/background field for the present local fracture-initiation model rather than the result being reproduced.

## Editorial one-sentence novelty statement

> The manuscript extends the exact elastic solution for a stationary contacting V-shaped interfacial shear crack to a local process-zone formulation for **corner branching initiation**, yielding closed analytical zone characteristics, admissibility conditions, and competing-mechanism criteria that are absent from the prior crack-tip-kinking and branch-tip-equilibrium formulations.
