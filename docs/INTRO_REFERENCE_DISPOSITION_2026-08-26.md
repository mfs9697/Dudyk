# Introduction reference disposition — 2026-08-26

## Purpose

This note records the scientific/editorial disposition of the 48 references in Dudyk's latest manuscript (`! Стаття (оновлена).docx`) after the sentence-level citation audit, the independent literature sweep, and the integrated rewrite of the Introduction.

The revised Introduction is **not** intended to preserve every citation from the author draft. The selection criterion is whether a source performs a necessary job in the argument

`corner singularity -> corner fracture initiation -> interface-crack kinking/process zone -> broken-interface corner models -> Nazarenko--Kipnis limiting equilibrium -> present post-instability branch model`.

Non-use in the Introduction therefore does not imply that a paper is scientifically weak. In particular, some references are retained for the problem statement or derivation, while others are omitted because a more direct primary source already supports the same sentence.

## Editorial rules used

1. Central novelty claims are anchored to **primary papers**, not to broad reviews.
2. A compact Introduction should not use a long citation block merely to demonstrate that a literature exists.
3. Isotropic plane-strain corner mechanics is the main lineage; anisotropic, axisymmetric, thermal, generalized-continuum, and indentation papers are retained only when they materially sharpen the present problem.
4. The wedge-indentation literature is not treated as a direct predecessor. After the final scientific sweep even the short analogy was removed because the much closer bimaterial-corner and process-zone literature already provides the required physical bridge.
5. The strongest novelty protection comes from citing the **closest competing/preceding models**, including the authors' own work, rather than omitting them.
6. References used only for local symmetry, asymptotic matching, energy definitions, or Wiener--Hopf machinery belong in the technical sections rather than in the Introduction.

The addition of these papers is the main reason a substantial fraction of Dudyk's 48 references is no longer necessary in the Introduction: the revised set is organized by **scientific role**, not by preservation of the historical bibliography.

## Resulting novelty boundary

The final Introduction no longer claims that corner fracture, kinking, or process-zone formation is generally unexplored. The defended gap is the specific sequence

`unstable limiting equilibrium of symmetric contacting interface shear cracks at a broken-interface corner -> small tensile Mode-I-type process zone in one constituent -> secondary branch initiation`.

This configuration-specific statement survives comparison with the closest literature found in the independent sweep and is substantially more defensible than a broad absence claim.
