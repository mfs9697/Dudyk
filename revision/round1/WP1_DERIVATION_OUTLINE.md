# Derivation outline for Sections 3.1–3.4

1. Choose the face displacement positive into the material. Subtract the stationary outer field. Its inner traction moment is subtracted as $CQ_i d_i^\lambda/(p+1+\lambda)$. The correction tail decays sufficiently fast to define the exterior transform for $\Re p<0$.
2. In each sector use the Airy angular modes of frequencies $1\pm p$. Set shear to zero at both ends, prescribe both end tractions, and compute the two normal gradients. Enforce zero gradient on the inactive bisector and continuity of normal displacement at contact. The resulting active traction equals $-(D_i/D)$ times the active gradient. The endpoint matrix and its scalar reduction appear in the accompanying audit.
3. Factor the positive even imaginary-axis symbol with the upward Cauchy contour. Its jump is $G_i^+/G_i^-$ and reflection gives $G_i^-(x)=1/G_i^+(-x)$. Reflection of the gamma functions gives $K^+K^-=p\cot(\pi p)$.
4. Multiply the traction equation by $L=K^+/(pG_i^+)$. For each forcing term $f_b/(p+b)$, assign $f_b[L(p)-L(-b)]/(p+b)$ to the left and transfer the simple residue term to the right. The common analytic continuation decays and is zero. Equality of the two residue coefficients eliminates the inverse-square-root stress and determines the free endpoint.
5. With $a=1+\lambda$ and $A=\sigma_iK^+(-1)/G_i^+(-1)$, the interior transform reduces to $\lambda A K^-G_i^-/[(p+1)(p+a)]$. The zero moment contains $G_i^-(0)=1/\sqrt{G_i(0)}$, and the first moment contains $G_i^-(1)=1/G_i^+(-1)$. Integration of the face gradient gives the opening and work. At fixed cohesive resistance $W_i$ is quadratic in equilibrium length, so $J_i=2W_i/d_i$.
